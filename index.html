About me

For five years I have been making radios listen for less power. A sensor node normally has to choose between being reachable and lasting a year on a battery; a wake-up receiver removes that choice, and my work is the RF front end that gives it back its range.

I work at HTWK Leipzig, where I take designs from concept through ADS simulation and PCB layout to a prototype measured on the bench. I also publish, teach seminars and supervise graduation projects. I am currently looking for a role in industrial R&D or project leadership.
