RF & wireless sensor networks

FUBE — radio sensors for space-based animal tracking

2022 – 2026 · HTWK Leipzig, funded by BMBF/VDI · multi-partner consortium

Lightweight radio sensors small enough to be carried by animals and heard from orbit. I was responsible for the wake-up receiver RF front end.

  • Designed ultra-low-power LNAs and matching networks for deep-signal detection, optimising sensitivity for long-range space-to-sensor links
  • Ran RF simulation in ADS and PCB prototyping in Altium Designer and Fusion 360 through to laboratory validation
  • Provided the measurement basis the team used for Doppler-based single-satellite localisation

RF design 45%PCB layout 20%Measurement 25%Consortium work 10%

Effort share, estimated

Expertise
Ultra-low-power LNA design, matching networks for deep-signal detection, noise figure and sensitivity budgeting, link budget for space-to-sensor paths, controlled 50 Ω routing, return-path and reference-plane discipline; antenna and link simulations directed with students
Tools
Keysight ADS, Ansys HFSS, Altium Designer, Fusion 360 Electronics, VNA, spectrum analyser, oscilloscope

WakeUp-Receiver

2021 – present · HTWK Leipzig, Smart Diagnostics and Online Monitoring group

The group's long-running effort to keep a sensor node continuously reachable on an average power budget of about 10 µW. Several circuit variants were analysed, built and measured; my part was the RF front end and the amplifiers that set its sensitivity.

  • Contributed a front end operating below 10 µW while meeting sensitivity targets
  • 868 MHz LNA and matching network design, schematic and PCB layout, prototype build
  • Bench characterisation with an MSP430-based test setup, including protocol scenarios

RF design 40%PCB layout 20%Measurement 25%Firmware 15%

Effort share, estimated

Expertise
868 MHz LNA and matching network design, power budget below 10 µW, impedance control, decoupling and rail design, multi-layer mixed-signal layout, bench characterisation, protocol scenarios
Tools
Keysight ADS, Altium Designer, MSP430, bare-metal C, VNA, spectrum analyser, logic analyser, JTAG/SWD
